# GF180MCU (gf180mcuD) and Tiny Tapeout GF

The rest of the skill is written for sky130. These are the facts and traps that differ on gf180mcuD, from
gf-433-oscillator (a 433.92 MHz VCO, TX driver and FSK receiver on ttgf26c, Nov 2026). Its `docs/platform.md` cites a
source for each.

## Process
- **No MIM caps on TT GF.** Projects get no Metal5, and gf180mcuD's MIM (option B) needs Metal5 for its top plate. Large
  caps are MOS gate caps, about 4 fF/µm² for the 3.3 V oxide (24 × 10 × 10 µm nfet_03v3 ≈ 9.8 pF). No inductors either.
- **Hi-res poly is 1 kΩ/sq** (`ppolyf_u_1k`). The `_2k` / `_3k` models in the ngspice library are other process
  options, not available; magic extracts only `_1k`. A 1 MΩ resistor is 1 × 970 µm.
- **Silicon vs extracted tt is about 1.0** for ring oscillators (ttgf0p2 ring #614, six inv_2 taps: 0.993-1.001,
  between the sf and tt corners; worst point 0.935). sky130 was 0.866. Inverter delay is about 51 ps per stage (tt,
  27 °C, 3.3 V).
- **EM:** the tech LEF (no temperature stated) gives Metal1-4 0.67 mA/µm, Metal5 1.5 mA/µm, vias 0.18 mA per cut. A
  looser "85 °C" bound used in practice: 2.09 mA/µm and 0.58 mA per cut. Size against the 85 °C bound and treat the
  LEF figure as a flag; state which one you used.
- Metal1-4 sheet R about 0.09 Ω/sq.

## Tools
- **KLayout gf180mcu DRC needs `feol=true beol=true`**, or the deck can silently run zero rules. Print the rules that
  ran and check the count.
- **Extract with magic 8.3.642**: 8.3.684 drops substrate C (as on sky130).
- **The ngspice models give one mismatch draw per instance, ignoring `m`.** Split matched devices into unit instances
  for Monte Carlo, or the mismatch of an m = 8 device is that of one finger.
- `tools/osic-mac` (an IIC-OSIC-TOOLS Docker wrapper) doesn't pass env vars into the container: use
  `osic-mac env K=V cmd`.

## Tiny Tapeout GF tile
- 1x2 = 346.64 × 325.36 µm (about 3× a sky130 1x2); 2x2 = 711.20 × 325.36 µm.
- Analog pins (`ua`) are Metal4 pads on the bottom edge, digital pins on the top. Power is vertical Metal4 stripes,
  VDPWR / VGND, plus VAPWR with the `_pgvaa` template.
- **VAPWR and VIO are independent of VDPWR** (3.3-5 V each, VAPWR ≤ VIO). Signals between VAPWR and VDPWR domains need
  level shifters unless both are 3.3 V. On the demo board both are 3.3 V, but separate pins: quiet analog on VAPWR,
  noisy digital and drivers on VDPWR is a free split.
- **TT's power switches:** VDPWR `tt_pg_3v3_hp_2` about 1.3 Ω (1.18 ff … 1.48 ss), VAPWR `tt_pg_5v0_2` about 2.7 Ω,
  each common to the whole tile. A 10 mA switching load moves everything else on that supply by ~13-27 mV.
- **The `ua` pin path:** the analog switch 11-37 Ω (tt; ss peak 72 Ω) plus 15-60 Ω of routing, and about 7.7 pF on the
  project node with the pin open (3 other projects on the pin). At 434 MHz into 50 Ω it loses 0.5-0.9 dB against DC.
- **Digital pins toggle cleanly to about 50 MHz** (measured on ttgf0p2: full swing at 23.7 MHz, still counting at
  49.7 MHz with ringing). Divide anything faster before a `uo_out` pin.
